## What we collect

- **Account:** when you sign in with Apple or Google we store the identifier and email address they share with us, so you can sign in again and we can find your data.
- **Your content:** the things you create in the app are stored so they are there when you come back, on this device or another.
- **Purchases:** if you buy something, RevenueCat processes the purchase on our behalf and tells us whether your subscription is active. We never see your payment details.
- **Ads:** if the app shows ads, Google AdMob may use advertising identifiers, subject to the consent you give in the app.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools beyond those listed here.

## Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and is only accessible to your signed-in account.

## Deleting your data

Delete your account from the app's settings screen, or see the [account deletion page](./delete-account.md). Deletion removes your account and everything stored against it.
