# Delete your {{APP_NAME}} account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

## In the app

1. Open the settings screen.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account and everything stored against it are deleted immediately. An active subscription is not cancelled by this: manage it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

## By email

If you can no longer open the app, email **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}** from the address you signed in with and ask for your account to be deleted. We will confirm when it is done.
